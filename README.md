# 🪖 Smart Helmet

### AI-Powered Road Safety & Smart Monitoring System

## 🚨 The Problem

For a rider, an accident can become life-threatening not only because of the impact, but because **help may not reach them in time**.

At the same time, potholes, traffic violations, and illegal garbage dumping create unsafe roads, but detecting and reporting these issues manually is difficult and inefficient.

**What if a helmet could detect danger, understand what is happening, and take action automatically?**

## 💡 Our Solution

**Smart Helmet** is an AI-powered safety and road-monitoring system that transforms a helmet into an intelligent companion for riders.

Our system combines **AI, computer vision, IoT sensors, and a mobile application** to detect road hazards, respond to accidents, and report violations and civic issues.

## ⚡ Key Features

- 🆘 **Accident Detection & Impact Analysis**  
  Detects falls and crashes, analyzes impact severity, and automatically sends SOS alerts with the rider's location to **family and emergency services**.

- 🕳️ **Pothole Detection**  
  Detects potholes on the road and identifies hazardous road conditions.

- 🚦 **Traffic Violation Detection & Reporting**  
  Detects helmet violations and captures vehicle number plates for automated reporting.

- 🗑️ **Garbage Detection & Reporting**  
  Identifies illegal garbage dumping and enables location-based reporting to the concerned authorities.

- 🎙️ **Voice Assistant**  
  Enables hands-free calling and basic interactions while keeping the rider focused on the road.

- 📱 **Mobile Application**  
  Provides emergency controls, alerts, incident records, location information, and safety monitoring.

- 🪖 **3D-Printed Prototype Helmet**  
  A functional **3D-printed helmet prototype** integrates the hardware and sensing components to demonstrate the complete system.

## 🛠️ Technology

**AI/ML:** Computer Vision, YOLOv8, TensorFlow Lite  
**IoT & Edge:** Raspberry Pi, ESP32, IMU Sensors, GPS, Camera  
**Backend:** Python, Node.js  
**Mobile:** React Native  
**Prototype:** 3D-Printed Smart Helmet
