# 🪖 Smart Helmet — Intelligent Road Safety System

## 📌 The Problem
Motorcyclists are among the most vulnerable road users. When accidents occur, delays in emergency detection and response can lead to fatal consequences. Furthermore, enforcing critical safety measures like helmet-wearing is difficult to do manually at scale, resulting in preventable injuries and fatalities.

## 💡 Our Solution
The **Smart Helmet** is an intelligent road safety system that transforms a standard helmet into a proactive safety device. It aims to protect riders and simplify traffic monitoring through:

- **Real-time Telemetry & Monitoring**: Continuously tracks the rider's speed, location, and motion parameters to keep tabs on overall safety.
- **Fall & Crash Detection**: Automatically detects high-impact crashes and immediately sends emergency SOS alerts, locations, and calls to family members and emergency contacts.
- **Traffic Violation Detection**: Identifies whether riders are wearing helmets and automatically extracts license plate information of violators for instant reporting.
- **Live Video Streaming**: Supports live camera feeds from the helmet for real-time road monitoring and visual evidence.
- **Voice Assistant**: Allows riders to control basic features and receive assistance hands-free while keeping their eyes on the road.
